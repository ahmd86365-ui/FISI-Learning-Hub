MOST IMPORTANT GRAMMAR – GESAMTÜBERSICHT
==================================================

1. PRESENT SIMPLE
-----------------

Use:
routine, habits, facts, regular activities

I work.
He works.

Do you work?
Does he work?

I don't work.
He doesn't work.


2. DO / DOES
------------

I / you / we / they → DO

Do you work?

he / she / it → DOES

Does he work?

After does, the verb stays in the base form:

Does he work?
NOT:
Does he works?


3. NEGATIVE
-----------

do not = don't

does not = doesn't

I don't work.

He doesn't work.


4. THIRD PERSON -S
------------------

He works.
She works.
It works.

He manages.
She provides.
It connects.


5. WILL
--------

will + base verb

I'll install the software.

You'll need a fast internet connection.

We'll have enough money.


6. FIRST CONDITIONAL
--------------------

If + Present Simple + will + base verb

If you follow a link, you'll go to another page.

If we buy tablets, we'll have enough money.


7. RELATIVE PRONOUNS
--------------------

who → people

which → things

that → people / things

where → places


8. PURPOSE
----------

to + verb

I use a drive to back up my data.

for + noun

I back up my data for security.

so that + subject + verb

I use a webcam so that I can make video calls.


9. REASON
---------

because + sentence

I use open-source software because it's free.

LESSON 30 – INTERVIEW / FACHGESPRÄCH
==================================================

Question:

What do you do if a computer cannot connect to the network?

Good answer:

First, I would check the network cable or Wi-Fi connection. Then I would check the IP configuration. I would use ping to test connectivity. After that, I would check the default gateway and DNS configuration. Finally, I would check the network device and document the solution.

German:

Zuerst würde ich das Netzwerkkabel oder die WLAN-Verbindung überprüfen. Danach würde ich die IP-Konfiguration überprüfen. Ich würde Ping verwenden, um die Verbindung zu testen. Anschließend würde ich das Standard-Gateway und die DNS-Konfiguration überprüfen. Zum Schluss würde ich das Netzwerkgerät überprüfen und die Lösung dokumentieren.
